How to play

  1. Swipe or use the arrow keys to slide every tile at once.
  2. Two tiles with the same number merge into one when they touch.
  3. After every move a new 2 or 4 appears. Plan ahead so the board doesn't fill up.
  4. Make a 2048 tile to win, then keep going as far as you can.

Tip: keep your biggest tile in a corner and build towards it.
